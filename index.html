About Ambica Spring

Established in 1997, Ambica Spring Industries has been a leading manufacturer and supplier of industrial springs for over 25 years. With decades of experience, we have built a strong reputation for delivering high-quality, durable, and customized spring solutions across various industries.

Our expertise, combined with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, allows us to meet the evolving demands of the industry with precision and reliability. We adhere to international standards like IS, BS, ASTM, AISL, and DIN to ensure product excellence.
