Our Story

25+ Years of Excellence in Spring Manufacturing

Who We Are

Established in 1997, Ambica Spring Industries has been a leading manufacturer and supplier of industrial springs for over 25 years. With decades of experience, we have built a strong reputation for delivering high-quality, durable, and customized spring solutions across various industries.

Our expertise, combined with state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities, allows us to meet the evolving demands of the industry with precision and reliability. We serve diverse sectors including:

  • ✔ Mining & Crushers
  • ✔ Construction
  • ✔ Power Transmission
  • ✔ Elevators
  • ✔ Vibrating Screens
  • ✔ Process Control Valves

We adhere to international standards like IS, BS, ASTM, AISL, and DIN to ensure product excellence and customer satisfaction.
